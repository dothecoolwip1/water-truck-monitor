The Water Delivery app is used by [ORGANISATION NAME] water truck drivers to receive delivery jobs and record water delivered. It is not for the general public.

What the app collects

  • Location, including in the background, only while the driver is on duty (between "Go on duty" and "End shift"). It is used to show the truck on the dispatch map, record the route for each delivery, and log arrival at each delivery point automatically. Location is not collected when the driver is off duty.
  • Photos the driver takes of the truck's water meter, and optional signatures from the person receiving water.
  • Delivery records: litres delivered, meter readings, times, the name of the person receiving water, and notes.
  • Account details: the driver's name and work email, and a device token used to send new-job alerts.

How it is used

The information is used only to run water deliveries: dispatching jobs, keeping delivery records for billing, and checking water delivered against water drawn from the holding tank. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored
