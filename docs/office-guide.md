# Office guide

Sign in at the server address in any browser. Admins manage accounts; dispatchers do everything else.

## Taking a request
1. **Dispatch > New job.**
2. Search for the customer, pick the delivery point, enter litres (leave blank for "fill to full").
3. Pick a date and a driver to schedule it now, or leave them blank to put it in the **Requests** column for later.
4. Set **Urgent** if the customer is out of water. Urgent jobs show a red edge on the board and on the driver's phone.

## Planning the day
- The board shows one day at a time; use the arrows or the date box.
- **Drag** a request onto a driver's column to assign it. Drag cards up and down to set the order the driver sees them in. The driver's phone gets an alert.
- Drag a card back to **Requests** to unassign it. Jobs already under way can be reordered but not moved to another driver.
- The map shows today's delivery points (yellow: requested, blue: scheduled, purple: on the way, orange: on site, green: delivered) and the trucks on duty.

## Following up
- Click any card for the job page: what was delivered, the meter photo, signature, the GPS trail and a history of every change.
- **Delivery ticket (PDF)** prints or emails a ticket.
- **Repeat for today** copies a job, handy for regular customers.
- **Enter paper ticket** records a delivery by hand if a driver's phone died.
- **Cancel job** asks for a reason, which is kept in the history.

## End of day / billing
- **Reports** shows litres drawn from the tank against litres delivered for each day. A gap of a few percent is normal (water left in trucks, meter rounding). Look into a sudden or growing gap.
- **Download deliveries CSV** (on Reports or Jobs) gives one row per delivery with account code, litres and meter readings, ready for your accounting system.
