# Office guide

Sign in at the server address in any browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to open it like an app. Admins manage accounts; dispatchers do everything else.

## Taking a request
1. **Dispatch > New job.**
2. Search for the customer, pick the delivery point, enter litres (leave blank for "fill to full").
3. Pick a date and a driver to schedule it now, or leave them blank to put it in the **Requests** column for later.
4. Set **Urgent** if the customer is out of water. Urgent jobs show a red edge on the board and on the driver's phone.

## Planning the day
- The board shows one day at a time; use the arrows or the date box.
- On a computer or tablet, **drag** a request onto a driver's column to assign it (press and hold on a touch screen). Drag cards up and down to set the order the driver sees them in. The driver's phone gets an alert.
- Drag a card back to **Requests** to unassign it. Jobs already under way can be reordered but not moved to another driver.
- On a phone, pick a driver (or Requests) along the top, and use the **⋯** menu on a job to assign it, move it up or down, or send it back to Requests. **List / Map** switches to the map.
- The map shows satellite imagery with house outlines. Each job's house lights up in its status colour, and the pins show today's delivery points (yellow: requested, blue: scheduled, purple: on the way, orange: on site, green: delivered) and the trucks on duty.

## Placing a delivery point
Under **Customers**, add or edit a delivery point and tap the customer's house on the map. The point snaps to the middle of the house, and the address fills in from the map when one is known. **Streets** switches to a street map where the satellite photo is poor.

## Following up
- Click any card for the job page: what was delivered, the meter photo, signature, the GPS trail and a history of every change.
- **Delivery ticket (PDF)** prints or emails a ticket.
- **Repeat for today** copies a job, handy for regular customers.
- **Enter paper ticket** records a delivery by hand if a driver's phone died.
- **Cancel job** asks for a reason, which is kept in the history.

## End of day / billing
- **Reports** shows litres drawn from the tank against litres delivered for each day. A gap of a few percent is normal (water left in trucks, meter rounding). Look into a sudden or growing gap.
- **Download deliveries CSV** (on Reports or Jobs) gives one row per delivery with account code, litres and meter readings, ready for your accounting system.
