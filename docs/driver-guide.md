# Driver guide

## Start of shift
1. Open **Water Delivery** and sign in (once; it remembers you).
2. Tap **Go on duty**, pick your truck, check the meter reading, tap **Go on duty**.
3. Allow location **Always** / **All the time** when asked. Location is only recorded while you're on duty.

## Each delivery: follow the big blue button
1. Tap the job, then **Start job**. Directions open in your maps app.
2. When you pull up, arrival is logged by itself. If not, tap **I've arrived**.
3. Fill the customer's tank, then tap **Record delivery**:
   - Type the **meter end** number. Litres work themselves out from the start number.
   - **Take photo** of the meter.
   - Optional: name and signature of whoever received the water, notes.
   - **Save delivery.**

## Filling the truck
Tap **Fill the truck**, then **Start filling** when water starts flowing and **Truck is full** when done.

## No signal?
Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone. The bar at the top says how many items are waiting and sends them as soon as you're back in range. Don't sign out while items are waiting.

## End of shift
Tap **End shift** on the main screen, enter the meter reading, confirm.

## Problems
If the bar turns red, tap it: it shows anything the office didn't accept (for example, a job the office cancelled). Tell the office, then clear the list.
